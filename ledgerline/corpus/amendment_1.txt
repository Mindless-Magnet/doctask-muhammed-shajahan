AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO THE MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This amendment is dated 2025-06-15 and takes effect on 2025-07-01.

The unit price for palletised freight is reduced to 145.00 per pallet with effect from
2025-07-01. The rate of 160.00 per pallet ceases to apply from that date.

All other terms of the Master Services Agreement remain unchanged.
