MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement is entered into between Harborview Foods Limited (the "Client")
and Calder Freight Services (the "Vendor").

Effective date: 2025-01-01. Initial term: 12 months from the effective date.

1. CHARGES
The Vendor shall invoice the Client at the rates set out in the applicable rate card.

2. PAYMENT
Payment terms are net 60 days from the date of a valid invoice.

3. LIABILITY
The aggregate liability cap is USD 250,000 for the term of this agreement.

4. RENEWAL
This agreement renews automatically for successive 12 month periods unless either party gives
60 days notice in writing before the end of the then current term.
